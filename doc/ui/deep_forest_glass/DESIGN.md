## Brand & Style
The design system pairs organic depth with bioluminescent modernism. Designed specifically for focused language acquisition, it creates a calming nocturnal sanctuary that strips away digital fatigue while keeping learners intrinsically motivated. The atmospheric world blends the stillness of a secluded old-growth canopy with futuristic refractive surfaces, turning routine daily practice into an immersive evening ritual.

The visual direction merges **Atmospheric Glassmorphism** with **High-Contrast Neon Precision**. Key structural components exist as multi-layered translucent panes suspended over deep emerald shadows. Bioluminescent highlights guide micro-interactions, while warm solar gold accents reward progress, milestones, and streaks. The aesthetic feels serene yet tactile, premium, focused, and frictionless.

## Colors
The palette balances low-light optical comfort with vivid functional signifiers:

- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** Base canvas rests in deepest obsidian emerald (`#022C22`). Deeper nested backdrops and inset surfaces sink into `#011712`, while elevated structural containers build upon `#064E3B`.
- **Glass Surfaces:** Cards and floating panels utilize frosted layers of `rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.6)` with backdrop blur and rim illumination via `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Primary & Secondary Accents:** Dynamic emerald neon (`#10B981`) drives primary calls to action, correct response confirmations, and audio playback tracks. Radiant teal (`#14B8A6`) serves as an active secondary state for grammar tags, active selection outlines, and category badges.
- **Milestone & Streak Accent:** Solar gold (`#F59E0B` and `#FBBF24`) is reserved strictly for high-dopamine moments: streak counts, XP boosts, mastery badges, and celebratory review screens.
- **Typography & Icons:** Pure crisp white (`#F9FAFB`) provides uncompromising legibility for English vocabulary words and target sentences. Secondary explanatory text relies on soft mint (`#A7F3D0`), while metadata, timestamps, and pronunciation guides utilize muted sage (`#6EE7B7`) and cool neutral grey (`#9CA3AF`).

## Typography
**Plus Jakarta Sans** governs the system entirely. Its open counters, rounded apexes, and generous x-height preserve exceptional clarity during fast scanning, rapid drills, and late-night study sessions.

- **Vocabulary Prompts & Headlines:** Rendered in `700` and `800` weights with slight negative tracking to give words an authoritative, memorable anchor on the frosted surface.
- **Reading Passages & Dialogue:** Rendered in `body-lg` and `body-md` at `400` weight with relaxed vertical leading (`1.5` to `1.6`) to minimize visual crowding between sentence structures.
- **Grammar Tags, Phonetics & Transcriptions:** Rendered in `label-sm` or `label-md` with `600`/`700` weights and slight positive tracking (`+0.03em` to `+0.05em`) to ensure clear separation of linguistic symbols (IPA notations) without colliding with adjacent text.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout architecture leverages a 4-column fluid layout on mobile, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet devices and 12 columns on desktop web layouts.

- **Vertical Rhythm:** Built strictly on an 8pt base grid (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 32px).
- **Mobile Hand-Friendly Margins:** Screen boundaries start with compact `1rem` margins on phone form factors, maxing out interactive thumb reach for repetitive drill exercises, while central cards stay bound to a focused max width of `480px` to maintain optimal reading line length.
- **Card Padding Dynamics:** Primary learning exercise cards demand generous internal breathing room (`space-lg` or 24px) to prevent interactive option tiles from feeling dense against question prompts.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth does not rely on opaque dropshadows; instead, it is articulated through physical light dispersion, refraction, and translucent layering across four distinct strata:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep, immovable obsidian emerald (`#022C22`) illuminated occasionally by subtle, organic ambient blurs (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08), transparent 70%)`).
- **Level 1 (Surface Glass Cards):** Background fills of `rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.60)` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(160%)`. Outlined with a top-lit hairline border (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` on top and sides, softly bleeding to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` at the bottom edge).
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Tiles & Modals):** Semi-elevated controls, audio buttons, and vocabulary matching pills use `rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.85)` with a delicate outer bloom: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(2, 44, 34, 0.7), 0 0 16px 0 rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Neon Active / Focus Glow):** Correct selections and focused elements produce an active bioluminescent aura using `0 0 24px 2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)` and `0 0 8px 1px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.4)`. Streak cards emit a warm solar radiance with `0 0 24px 2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.

## Shapes
The interface embraces organic curvature without sacrificing architectural precision.

- **Primary Glass Cards & Modals:** Standardized with an explicit `24px` corner radius (`1.5rem`), creating a pebble-smooth feel that softens card edges against deep background hues.
- **Exercise Option Tiles & Input Fields:** Sized with a `12px` to `16px` border radius (`rounded-lg`), ensuring clear target zones that nest harmoniously inside 24px parent containers.
- **Chips, Audio Player Triggers, and Streak Badges:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), distinguishing rapid utility triggers and status metrics from content panels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action / Check):** Solid neon emerald (`#10B981`) fill with deep forest text (`#022C22`, `font-weight: 700`). Features an ambient neon rim glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`). Active state applies a subtle scale compress (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary (Listen / Repeat):** Frosted glass fill (`rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.7)`) with light mint text (`#A7F3D0`) and a crisp `1px` translucent boundary (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`).
- **Streak / Premium:** Solar gold fill (`#F59E0B`) with deep obsidian text (`#022C22`) and warm golden bloom (`0 4px 20px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`).

### Exercise Cards & Multiple-Choice Tiles
- **Base State:** Translucent glass container (`rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.6)`) with `24px` radius, backed by 20px blur and border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Selected State:** Glowing emerald contour (`border-color: #10B981`) and a background shift to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.
- **Correct State:** Vibrant emerald neon boundary (`#10B981`), white text, and a celebratory particle pulse.
- **Incorrect State:** Deep rose glass tint (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`), ruby border (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.6)`), and muted shake transition.

### Chips & Vocabulary Badges
- Compact pill-shaped metadata markers (`height: 32px`, `padding: 0 12px`).
- Inactive badges feature soft dark emerald backgrounds (`rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.5)`) with muted sage copy (`#6EE7B7`).
- Active grammatical category tags (e.g., "Noun", "Past Participle") switch to a teal edge (`#14B8A6`) with mint lettering (`#A7F3D0`).

### Audio & Pronunciation Waveform Controls
- Circular pill glass containers housing speaker or microphone icons.
- When audio is actively playing, soundwave visualizer bars pulse using primary emerald (`#10B981`) and secondary teal (`#14B8A6`), accompanied by a soft background blur halo.

### Input Fields (Fill-in-the-blank)
- Inset glass styling (`rgba(2, 44, 34, 0.6)`) with subtle inner shadow to signify recess.
- Active focus shifts the border from passive mint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`) to brilliant neon emerald (`#10B981`) accompanied by a 4px soft outer spread.
- Text input renders in crisp `#F9FAFB` with caret colored `#10B981`.

### Progress Bars & Streaks
- **Progress Track:** Recessed slot (`#011712`, height 8px, `border-radius: 999px`).
- **Progress Fill:** Fluid linear gradient (`#10B981` to `#14B8A6`) with an illuminated head pin glow.
- **Streak Header Counter:** Floating pill incorporating solar flame glyph, displaying current streak value in solar gold (`#FBBF24`) set against frosted dark emerald.
